A note from Vitalist Fresh

Thank you for being part of our journey.

Our café in Spring Park, Minnesota has closed, and Vitalist Fresh is no longer taking orders.

To everyone who dined with us on Lake Minnetonka, ordered pickup and delivery across the Twin Cities, or joined a regenerative cooking class: thank you. Sharing nourishing food with this community has been a gift.

Healing through food, since 2012.

Founded by chef Sarah Ann Haugen, Vitalist began as a small superfood line and grew into a test kitchen, a café on Melrose Avenue in Los Angeles, and finally our home on Lake Minnetonka.

Every recipe was built on the same idea: organic, plant-forward food that makes you feel good.
